### How to join

Head to [substack.com/notes](https://substack.com/notes) or find the “Notes” tab in the [Substack app](https://substack.com/app/app-store-redirect). As a subscriber to Memo, you’ll automatically see my notes. Feel free to like, reply, or share them around!
